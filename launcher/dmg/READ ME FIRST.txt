eidos launcher for macOS (Apple silicon)

1. Drag "eidos launcher" onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open Terminal (in Applications, Utilities), paste this line and press
   Return:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/eidos launcher.app"

3. Open eidos launcher from Applications.

Why step 2: the launcher is not signed by Apple, so macOS blocks it the
first time and says it cannot be opened. The command removes the
"downloaded from the internet" mark from this one app only. You do not
need to turn off Gatekeeper, and you do this once: the launcher updates
itself after that.

eidos is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by

game data: you need your own copy of Pokemon Black or White.
